WHITE LIFE LLC

Security engineering — tooling development, adversarial testing, and automation.

WHITE LIFE LLC is a small security engineering company. We build, test, and operate our own security tooling against lab hosts and public test environments: scanners, traffic capture and analysis utilities, offensive tooling and exploit experiments, and the automation that runs them.

What we do

  • Network and service discovery, fingerprinting, and inventory automation
  • Traffic capture, protocol analysis, and interception tooling
  • Offensive tooling and exploit validation against public lab targets
  • Build-and-test automation for security tooling pipelines
  • Model-assisted code generation for tooling, used as an accelerator on the above

How we work

Work is scoped to systems we own or to public test environments. Tooling is written to run unattended, and every change goes through a build and verification gate before it is deployed to our own hosts. Model-assisted coding is part of the tooling workflow, so review quality and reproducibility matter more than volume.

Scope of systems

Company-owned lab hosts and internal tooling, plus public lab targets and vendor test environments. Client-facing services are not offered at this time.

Status

Provisional entry under Cyber Verification Program. Certification evidence for ISO/IEC 27001 to follow within six months.
